QuickLook Next

Privacy policy

Effective August 17, 2026

Summary

QuickLook Next is an open-source Windows file previewer. It does not require an account, run advertising, operate a telemetry backend, sell personal information, or upload the files you preview to the QuickLook Next project.

Files and metadata

Files selected in Windows Explorer are read and processed on your device. Preview content and metadata, including image EXIF metadata, remain local unless you choose one of the external actions described below. Restricted helper processes may be used for parsing and rendering, but they run on the same device.

Local settings and diagnostics

The app stores preferences and bounded diagnostic logs in your local application data. Paths written to logs are redacted. Logs are rotated at approximately 4 MiB. A diagnostics archive is created only after you confirm the disclosure and choose where to save it. QuickLook Next does not upload that archive automatically.

Network access and third parties

  • When you manually check for updates, the app downloads a small signed-release metadata file from GitHub. GitHub may receive ordinary connection information, such as your IP address and the app's update-check user agent, under GitHub's privacy terms.
  • When you explicitly choose to open an image location in Google Maps, the app opens a Google Maps URL containing latitude and longitude read from that image's EXIF metadata. Those coordinates are then sent to Google by your browser.
  • For an online-only cloud file, the app asks for confirmation before requesting hydration through Windows and the file's configured cloud provider. The provider's own privacy terms apply to that download.
  • Links to GitHub, release downloads, and support open in your default browser and are governed by the destination service's privacy terms.

Retention and control

Settings and logs remain on your device until you remove them or Windows/app cleanup removes them. You can decline cloud-file downloads, avoid external map links, skip update checks, and choose not to create a diagnostics archive.

Children, changes, and contact

QuickLook Next is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children. This policy may be updated when app behavior changes; the effective date above identifies the current version. Questions or privacy requests can be filed through GitHub Issues.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 8 月 17 日

摘要

QuickLook Next 是开源 Windows 文件预览工具。它不要求账号，不展示广告，不运行遥测后端， 不出售个人信息，也不会把你预览的文件上传给 QuickLook Next 项目。

本地文件、设置和诊断

在资源管理器中选中的文件及其元数据仅在本机处理。应用会在本地应用数据目录保存设置和有界日志， 日志中的路径会被脱敏并在约 4 MiB 时轮换。只有在你确认说明并选择保存位置后，应用才会生成诊断包， 且不会自动上传该文件。

网络访问

  • 手动检查更新时，会从 GitHub 下载一份很小的发布元数据；GitHub 可能按其政策接收常规连接信息。
  • 只有你主动点击在 Google 地图中打开位置时，浏览器才会把图片 EXIF 中的经纬度发送给 Google。
  • 云端占位文件只有在你确认后，才会通过 Windows 和相应云服务提供商下载；该提供商的隐私条款适用。
  • GitHub、版本下载和支持链接会在默认浏览器中打开，并受目标服务的隐私条款约束。

控制与联系

你可以拒绝云文件下载、不使用外部地图链接、跳过更新检查，也可以不生成诊断包。 如有隐私问题或请求，请通过 GitHub Issues 联系项目维护者。
